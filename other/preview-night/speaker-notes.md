# Speaker Notes — CS 7180 Preview Night (5 minutes)

---

## Slide 1: Title (15 seconds)

"Hi everyone, I'm John Guerra Gomez from Khoury College. I'm here to tell you about CS 7180: Vibe Coding — AI-Assisted Software Engineering, offered this Fall at our Silicon Valley campus."

---

## Slide 2: The Hook (30 seconds)

"You've probably heard the term 'vibe coding' — just tell AI what to build and hope for the best. That's fine for prototypes, but employers want engineers who can ship production software. This course teaches you to use AI tools professionally — with testing, CI/CD, evaluations, and real engineering practices. AI speed with engineering quality."

---

## Slide 3: 3 AI Modalities (45 seconds)

"We cover three distinct ways to work with AI. First, conversational AI for architecture and brainstorming. Then AI-powered IDEs for your daily coding workflow — autocomplete, inline suggestions, rules files. Finally, agentic coding — where AI agents autonomously write, test, and refactor code across entire projects. You'll also learn prompt engineering, context engineering, and how to evaluate AI-generated code. And because this field moves fast, the curriculum adapts to the latest tools each semester."

---

## Slide 4: Course Topics (45 seconds)

"Here's the semester at a glance. We start with LLM fundamentals — how these models actually work, tokens, context windows, hallucinations. Then we move through all three AI modalities with hands-on practice. By the second half, you're building with autonomous agents, learning agent architectures, and working with the Agent SDK. The tech stack is what Silicon Valley uses: React, Next.js, Node, PostgreSQL, and GitHub Actions for CI/CD."

---

## Slide 5: 3 Portfolio Projects (45 seconds)

"The heart of the course is three projects — each one portfolio-worthy. Project 1 is solo: you build a personal utility app solving a real problem validated through user research. Project 2 is in pairs: a full-stack application with authentication, a public API, 80% test coverage, and a CI/CD pipeline. Project 3, also in pairs: a production-grade application with advanced architecture, monitoring, security audits, and AI evaluation systems. By the end, you have three deployed applications you can walk into any interview with."

---

## Slide 6: Evaluation & Format (30 seconds)

"Projects are 50% of your grade — they're the centerpiece. Five homework assignments scaffold toward the projects at 25%. Participation and weekly quizzes make up the rest. The course meets once per week for about three hours, right here at the Silicon Valley campus."

---

## Slide 7: Prerequisites & Tools (20 seconds)

"You need CS 5010 or CS 5004 — basic programming fundamentals. No AI experience required, we teach you everything. Tools run about $40 a month for Claude Pro and an AI-powered IDE."

---

## Slide 8: Outcomes (30 seconds)

"What do you walk away with? Three deployed apps for your portfolio. A professional AI development workflow that makes you faster and more effective. Full-stack skills in the exact tech stack companies are hiring for. And the confidence to use these tools in interviews and on the job from day one."
