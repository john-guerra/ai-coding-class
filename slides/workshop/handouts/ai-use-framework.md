# Your Personal AI-Use Framework — Worksheet

*Agentic Engineering workshop · take this with you*

The scarce skill isn't typing — it's **verification and judgment**. Two anchors to remember:

- **METR:** experienced devs were **~19% slower** with AI while *believing* they were 20% faster.
  Speed ≠ speedup; self-perception is unreliable. [source](https://metr.org/blog/2025-07-10-early-2025-ai-experienced-os-dev-study/)
- **The 70% problem:** AI nails the first 70% fast; the last 30% (edge cases, security, integration)
  is where the real work and cost hide.

## Anti-deskilling habits (keep your edge)

- [ ] **Attempt-first, then-AI** for anything you want to *learn*.
- [ ] **Explain the why** — after the agent solves it, restate the solution in your own words.
- [ ] **Scheduled no-AI practice** — periodic reps without the tool.
- [ ] **Mandatory human review** — the code author isn't the only reviewer.

> Collaborator, not a crutch.
