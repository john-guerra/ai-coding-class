The Project — Linkstash

One small app you drive an agent across, Sessions 2–4.

A link-saver you can hold in your head

Save URLs with a title, tags, and a private note; it auto-fetches each page's title, and you can share a digest. Small on purpose — real dependencies, a tested core, and a security surface that's there by design.

Node 20 · Express · SQLite · Vitest. A fresh clone tests green.
